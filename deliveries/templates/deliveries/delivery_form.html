Good delivery data matters

Accurate customer contact details and drop-off coordinates make exceptions easier to resolve and customer tracking more useful.

  • Include suburb and city in the address.
  • Set an ETA only when operations has a reasonable estimate.
  • Keep sensitive internal notes out of customer-visible fields.
